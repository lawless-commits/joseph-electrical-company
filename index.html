Frequently asked questions

Why does my earth leakage or breaker keep tripping?

Usually it's moisture in an outside plug or light, a faulty appliance such as a geyser element or kettle, damaged cabling, or too much load on one circuit. We isolate circuits one by one and test insulation resistance to find the cause. Don't keep resetting it or tape it up. The device trips to protect you.

What is a Certificate of Compliance (CoC) and when do I need one?

A CoC confirms that the fixed electrical installation meets SANS 10142-1. You'll normally need one when selling a property, after significant electrical work, and often for insurance. If the inspection finds faults, we quote for the remedial work before issuing the certificate.

Is my house ready for an inverter or backup system?

Most homes are, but it's usually best to split essential circuits (lights, Wi-Fi, fridge, TV) from heavy loads like the geyser and stove. We check the DB board, earthing and cable sizes first so the inverter is installed safely and sized correctly. Use the estimator above for a rough idea.

How quickly can you get to me?

Urgent faults are prioritised, often same day or next day depending on location and availability. Planned work is usually booked within a few days. (Demo copy. Confirm real turnaround times.)

Do you work for businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We do household callouts and also look after shops, offices and small workshops, with after-hours work, scheduled inspections and backup for tills and refrigeration. Choose "Commercial / Business" in the estimator or form so we can plan the right team and timing.
